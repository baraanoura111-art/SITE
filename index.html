The Oasis clusters at a glance

The Oasis has six launched clusters, from 4-bedroom villas to 7-bedroom mansions. Here's how they compare.

Palmiera

The first phase, released in four parts (Palmiera 1, 2, 3 and the Collective) with 418 villas in total. Mostly 4-bedroom, with 5-bedroom villas in Palmiera 1. BUA 5,627–8,689 sq ft; plots 7,948–13,723 sq ft. Handover from Q4 2026 to Q1 2029, depending on the phase.

Mirage

204 larger family villas with 5 and 6 bedrooms. BUA 10,225–12,967 sq ft; plots 9,804–23,052 sq ft. 90/10 payment plan, handover Q2 2028.

Lavita

The community's 49 mansions, with 6 and 7 bedrooms in four signature styles. BUA 19,012–29,915 sq ft; plots 21,056–40,821 sq ft. 80/20 plan, handover Q4 2028.

Marèva

The largest cluster, with 658 villas across Marèva and Marèva 2, in 4 to 6 bedrooms. BUA 7,254–12,986 sq ft; plots 7,790–24,015 sq ft. 80/20 plan, handover Q1 2030.

Address Villas – Tierra and Palace Villas – Ostra

The two branded clusters: 487 Address villas and 526 Palace villas, both with 4 to 6 bedrooms, BUA 7,269–12,959 sq ft and 80/20 plans. Tierra hands over in Q2 2029, Ostra in Q3 2029.

Handover dates are as per the developer's schedule and may change.

What it costs to buy a villa in The Oasis

On top of the purchase price, buying a villa in The Oasis comes with a few standard fees. Here's what to expect.

The fees

  • DLD transfer fee: 4% of the purchase price.
  • Title deed fee (ready villas): AED 580, or an Oqood registration fee of AED 90 for off-plan resales.
  • Trustee office fee: AED 4,200 including VAT for ready villas, or AED 5,250 for off-plan.
  • Agency fee: 2% + VAT.

For an off-plan resale, you pay the seller what they've paid the developer plus any agreed premium, and continue the remaining payment plan with Emaar.

Documents you'll need

  • Emirates ID
  • Passport
  • Visa
  • Mortgage pre-approval, if you're financing
  • A security deposit cheque for 10% of the purchase price

Figures are indicative. Mortgage buyers pay additional registration and bank fees. Use the purchase fee calculator to see the full breakdown for any price.

FAQs

What are the key highlights of The Oasis by Emaar?

Stunningly designed villas, a gated community for tranquillity, a prime location with seamless connectivity, vibrant community engagement, meticulously crafted water bodies including lagoons, and exceptional amenities.

The Oasis is strategically located with easy access to key destinations such as Burj Khalifa, Downtown Dubai, Dubai International Airport, Sheikh Zayed Road and The Dubai Mall.

What types of property are available?

Spacious villas and mansions with 4 to 7 bedrooms, from about 5,600 sq ft to over 34,000 sq ft, across ten launched clusters.

How is the connectivity?

The Oasis sits on Sheikh Zayed bin Hamdan Road (D54), close to Emirates Road (E611) and the planned extension of Al Khail Road (E44), with around 18 minutes to Al Maktoum International Airport.

Are there any schools near The Oasis?

Yes. The Oasis is conveniently located near reputable schools and educational institutions, giving families access to quality education options for their children.

What documents do I need to buy a property?

Your Emirates ID, passport and visa, a mortgage pre-approval if you're financing, and a security deposit cheque for 10% of the purchase price.

What costs come on top of the price?

Mainly the DLD transfer fee, registration and trustee fees, agency commission, and the developer's NOC for resale units. I'll send a full cost breakdown for any villa you're considering.

Are prices negotiable?

On resale, often yes. Emaar's off-plan prices are usually fixed, but resale deals leave room, especially with good market data behind the offer.

Do you help after the deal closes?

Yes: handover snagging, renovation, leasing and management, plus business setup and residency if you need them.
