Emaar's Flagship Lagoon Community

The Oasis is Emaar's resort-style villa community in Dubailand, launched in June 2023. Villas and mansions sit around swimmable lagoons, sandy beaches and flowing canals, in a low-density, gated masterplan built for privacy, space and family life.

FAQs

What are the key highlights of The Oasis by Emaar?

Stunningly designed villas, a gated community for tranquillity, a prime location with seamless connectivity, vibrant community engagement, meticulously crafted water bodies including lagoons, and exceptional amenities.

The Oasis is strategically located with easy access to key destinations such as Burj Khalifa, Downtown Dubai, Dubai International Airport, Sheikh Zayed Road and The Dubai Mall.

Explore the community
Are there any schools near The Oasis?

Yes. The Oasis is conveniently located near reputable schools and educational institutions, giving families access to quality education options for their children.

Read the schools guide
What types of property are available?

Spacious villas and mansions with 4 to 7 bedrooms, from about 5,600 sq ft to over 34,000 sq ft, across ten launched clusters.

Search in the Oasis Villa Finder
How is the connectivity?

The Oasis sits on Sheikh Zayed bin Hamdan Road (D54), close to Emirates Road (E611) and the planned extension of Al Khail Road (E44), with around 18 minutes to Al Maktoum International Airport.

See the location
What documents do I need to buy a property?

Your Emirates ID, passport and visa, a mortgage pre-approval if you're financing, and a security deposit cheque for 10% of the purchase price.

How buying a villa works
What costs come on top of the price?

Mainly the DLD transfer fee, registration and trustee fees, agency commission, and the developer's NOC for resale units. I'll send a full cost breakdown for any villa you're considering.

Open the purchase fee calculator
Are prices negotiable?

On resale, often yes. Emaar's off-plan prices are usually fixed, but resale deals leave room, especially with good market data behind the offer.

Do you help after the deal closes?

Yes: handover snagging, renovation, leasing and management, plus business setup and residency if you need them.

Overview

The second Marèva phase, between Emirates Road and Yalayis Street, with its own school, sports courts, event lawns and urban canals.

Marèva 2 has 346 villas with 4–6 bedrooms in Chamfered, Contemporary and Classical. Built-up areas range from 7,254–12,986 sq ft on plots of 7,790–23,414 sq ft. 319 of the 346 villas have a basement. The payment plan is 80/20, with handover expected in Q1 2030.
