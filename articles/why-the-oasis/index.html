Why The Oasis?
Why I Chose To Specialize Here

2 min read · By Baraa Noura · Updated 9 October 2026

The Oasis by Emaar is a flagship luxury villa community in Dubai, built around a simple idea: more space, more privacy and a lower-density way of living.

Unlike the high-rise developments Dubai is often known for, The Oasis is centered on standalone villas, generous plots, landscaped surroundings and lagoons. It's designed for buyers looking for a private home rather than simply a property within a large residential development.

A Flagship Emaar Community

Emaar is one of the developers most closely associated with the transformation of Dubai, and describes itself as a builder of the city, with communities and landmarks that have helped shape it.

The Oasis represents that vision at a different scale: a low-density luxury villa community designed around space, greenery, water and privacy. For buyers looking at luxury villas in Dubai, this combination is what makes The Oasis particularly interesting.

Why I Chose The Oasis

My background has always been in villas and townhouses, so when I looked at The Oasis, specializing in the community felt like a natural fit.

The combination of standalone villas, large plots, low-density living and Emaar's master planning creates a community where the details of each property matter. Villa position, plot, orientation, views, privacy and proximity to different parts of the community can all influence which property makes sense for a particular buyer.

Rather than trying to cover every development in Dubai, I chose to focus my expertise on The Oasis before handover. That lets me understand the community as it takes shape, follow the available villa options closely and help buyers make better-informed decisions before they receive the keys.

One Community. One Specialist.

Buying a villa is a very different decision from buying an apartment. The plot, surroundings, privacy, orientation and relationship with the wider community all become part of the decision.

That's why my approach is simple: understand what you're looking for, identify the right options within The Oasis, and guide you from shortlist to handover. If you're considering buying a villa in The Oasis Dubai, I'm here to help you navigate the community and find the property that fits your requirements.

One community. One specialist. From shortlist to handover.
